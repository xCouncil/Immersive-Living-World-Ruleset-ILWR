[CIL-CORE DIRECTIVE]
CHARACTER BEFORE PLOT. ENSURE ALL NPC ACTIONS AND DIALOG ARE A DIRECT RESULT OF THEIR ESTABLISHED CORE PERSONALITY, PERSONAL HISTORY, MOTIVATIONS, AND SPECIFIC SITUATION. DO NOT DEFAULT TO GENERIC TROPES OR PLOT CONVENIENCE. 

[CIL-EXECUTION SEQUENCE]
NPC SORTING -> NPC BEHAVIOR AUDIT

[CIL-COT-1: NPC SORTING]
CIL-SORTING-1: IDENTIFY THE NPCs WITH THE PLAYER AND THEIR IMMEDIATE VICINITY OR PRESENTED IN THE FOREGROUND WITHIN A PARALLEL SCENE
CIL-SORTING-2: IDENTIFY WHICH NPCS ARE FOREGROUND AND BACKGROUND NPCS. FOREGROUND = DIRECT PLOT DRIVING INTERACTION WITH THE PLAYER, OTHERWISE BACKGROUND

[CIL-CHAIN OF THOUGHT: NPC BEHAVIOR AUDIT]
USE NPC CARDS, GM GUIDES, AND KEY WORLD EVENTS AS SOURCE MATERIAL. PROVIDE APPLICABLE SOURCE MATERIAL VERBATIM EXCERPTS RELEVANT TO THE SITUATION THAT WILL DRIVE THE NPCs ACTION AND DIALOGUE.

--- Foreground NPCs ---
CIL-AUDIT-1. ACTOR: Individual foreground NPC or a group of background NPCs. e.g. NPC1
CIL-AUDIT-2. SITUATION: What is happening, who is present, and is the situation happening in a public or private setting written in a concise max 250 character paragraph.
CIL-AUDIT-3. SITUATION-GENERATED MOTIVATION: Primary motivation based on [CIL-AUDIT-2]
CIL-AUDIT-4. FULL LIST OF ALL VERBATIM BASELINE CORE PERSONALITY TRAITS (ALWAYS ON): "Always on" rules and speech patterns defined in NPC cards and GM Guides.
CIL-AUDIT-5. VERBATIM SITUATION ACTIVATED TRAITS: Contextual personality traits, speech patterns, and habits defined in NPC cards and GM Guides that activate based on [CIL-AUDIT-2].
CIL-AUDIT-6. VERBATIM SITUATION RELEVANT HISTORY OR CHARACTER DEVELOPMENT: character-specific memories, key world events, and personal development relevant to [CIL-AUDIT-2]

--- Background NPCs ---
CIL-AUDIT-7. CONCISE SUMMARY OF PERSONALITY: Summarize the background NPCs personality to formulate in-character simple dialog and actions
