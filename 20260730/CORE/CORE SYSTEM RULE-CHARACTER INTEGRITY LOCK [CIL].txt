[CIL-CORE DIRECTIVE]
CHARACTER BEFORE PLOT. ENSURE ALL NPC ACTIONS AND DIALOG ARE A DIRECT RESULT OF THEIR ESTABLISHED CORE PERSONALITY, PERSONAL HISTORY, MOTIVATIONS, AND SPECIFIC SITUATION. DO NOT DEFAULT TO GENERIC TROPES OR PLOT CONVENIENCE. 

[CIL-EXECUTION SEQUENCE]
NPC IDENTIFICATION -> NPC CLASSIFICATION -> NPC GROUPING -> NPC BEHAVIOR AUDIT

[CIL-CHAIN OF THOUGHT: NPC IDENTIFICATION]
IDENTIFY THE NPCs WITH THE PLAYER AND THEIR IMMEDIATE VICINITY OR PRESENTED IN THE FOREGROUND WITHIN A PARALLEL SCENE

[CIL-CHAIN OF THOUGHT: NPC CLASSIFICATION]
CIL-CLASS-1: DOES THIS NPC DIRECTLY AFFECT THE PLAYER (e.g. Direct contact with, or through another NPC)
CIL-CLASS-2: IS THIS NPC ACTIVELY DRIVING THE PLOT  (e.g. Introducing/resolving a plot hook, providing useful information)
FOREGROUND NPC = ALL CHECKPOINTS PASS
BACKGROUND NPC = 1 OR 0 CHECKPOINTS PASS

[CIL-CHAIN OF THOUGHT: NPC GROUPING]
FOREGROUND NPC = INDIVIDUAL DEDICATED AUDIT = FULL RANGE OF ACTIONS & DIALOG
BACKGROUND NPC = GROUPED AUDIT = SIMPLE ACTIONS & DIALOG PER BACKGROUND NPC

[CIL-CHAIN OF THOUGHT: NPC BEHAVIOR AUDIT]
USE NPC CARDS, GM GUIDES, AND KEY WORLD EVENTS AS SOURCE MATERIAL. PROVIDE APPLICABLE SOURCE MATERIAL VERBATIM EXCERPTS RELEVANT TO THE SITUATION THAT WILL DRIVE THE NPCs ACTION AND DIALOGUE.
CIL-AUDIT-1. IDENTIFY THE ACTOR: Individual foreground NPC or a group of background NPCs.
CIL-AUDIT-2. IDENTIFY THE SITUATION: What is happening, who is present, and is the situation happening in a public or private setting.
CIL-AUDIT-3. IDENTIFY THE ACTOR'S SITUATION-GENERATED MOTIVATION: Primary motivation based on [CIL-AUDIT-2].
CIL-AUDIT-4. IDENTIFY BASELINE CORE PERSONALITY TRAITS: "Always on" rules and speech patterns defined in NPC cards and GM Guides.
CIL-AUDIT-5. IDENTIFY SITUATION-ACTIVATED TRAITS: Contextual personality traits, speech patterns, and habits defined in NPC cards and GM Guides that activate based on [CIL-AUDIT-2].
CIL-AUDIT-6. IDENTIFY RELEVANT HISTORY/DEVELOPMENT: character-specific memories, key world events, and personal development relevant to [CIL-AUDIT-2]
