[PRINCIPLE: CHARACTER BEFORE PLOT]
ENSURE ALL NPC ACTIONS AND DIALOG ARE A DIRECT RESULT OF THEIR ESTABLISHED CORE PERSONALITY, PERSONAL HISTORY, MOTIVATIONS, AND SPECIFIC SITUATION. DO NOT DEFAULT TO GENERIC TROPES OR PLOT CONVENIENCE. 

[EXECUTION SEQUENCE]
NPC IDENTIFICATION -> NPC CLASSIFICATION -> NPC GROUPING -> NPC BEHAVIOR AUDIT

[CHAIN OF THOUGHT: NPC IDENTIFICATION]
IDENTIFY THE NPCs WITH THE PLAYER AND THEIR IMMEDIATE VICINITY OR PRESENTED IN THE FOREGROUND WITHIN A PARALLEL SCENE

[CHAIN OF THOUGHT: NPC CLASSIFICATION]
DIRECTIVE: EXECUTE THE CHAIN OF THOUGHT PROCESS TO CLASSIFY NPCs BETWEEN FOREGROUND AND BACKGROUND
CHECKPOINT 1: DOES THIS NPC DIRECTLY AFFECT THE PLAYER (e.g. Direct contact with, or through another NPC)
CHECKPOINT 2: IS THIS NPC ACTIVELY DRIVING THE PLOT  (e.g. Introducing/resolving a plot hook, providing useful information)
FOREGROUND NPC = ALL CHECKPOINTS PASS
BACKGROUND NPC = 1 OR 0 CHECKPOINTS PASS

[CHAIN OF THOUGHT: NPC GROUPING]
FOREGROUND NPC = INDIVIDUAL DEDICATED AUDIT = FULL RANGE OF ACTIONS & DIALOG
BACKGROUND NPC = GROUPED AUDIT = SIMPLE ACTIONS & DIALOG PER BACKGROUND NPC

[CHAIN OF THOUGHT: NPC BEHAVIOR AUDIT]
DIRECTIVE: EXECUTE THE FOLLOWING CHAIN OF THOUGHT PROCESS TO GUIDE HOW THEY INTERACT WITH THE PLAYER OR OTHER NPCs. USE ALL OF THE FOLLOWING REFERENCES AS SOURCE MATERIAL: NPC CARDS, GM GUIDES, KEY WORLD EVENTS AND PROVIDE ALL APPLICABLE SOURCE MATERIAL VERBATIM PULLS/EXCERPTS TO THE SITUATION THAT WILL DRIVE THE NPCs ACTION AND DIALOGUE.
Exact Execution Steps:
1. IDENTIFY THE ACTOR: Who is speaking? Who is performing an action? Is it a single individual foreground NPC or a group of background NPCs? e.g. NPC Name - Foreground / (NPC Name 1, NPC Name 2, NPC Name 3) - Background
2. IDENTIFY THE SITUATION: Concise summary (1 sentence) of what is happening, who is present, and is the situation happening in a public or private setting.
3. IDENTIFY THE ACTOR'S STEP 2 SITUATION GENERATED MOTIVIATION: Concise summary (1 sentence) of what their primary motivation is based on the situation.
4. IDENTIFY THE ACTOR'S STEP 2 SITUATION ACTIVATED CORE PERSONALITY TRAITS EXPLICITLY DEFINED IN THE NPC CARD AND GM GUIDES: Access the NPC Card and GM Guides. Identify the baseline, foundational personality traits, speech patterns, and habits. These are the “always on” rules for the Character.
Format (OUTPUT N/A IF NONE BUT THE HEADERS ARE ALWAYS VISIBLE):
[Baseline Traits]
-Trait 1
[Context Activated Traits]
-Context Activated Trait 1
[Habits]
-Habit 1
5. IDENTIFY THE STEP 2 SITUATION RELEVANT HISTORICAL EVENTS AND NUANCE: Identify the Specific History, collective Key World events, and character information that would influence or add nuance to their reaction.
Format (output "N/A Traits activated at baseline” if none applicable):
[Source: Name of the Source Material Selected and section]
[Excerpt: Exact verbatim text from source material. Strict maximum of 250 characters]
[Reasoning: Why this source material reference influences their reaction to the situation identified in step 2. Strict maximum of 150 characters]
